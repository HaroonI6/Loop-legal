# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

loop is built to know you well enough to be useful, and to keep that knowledge between you and loop. this is a plain-language description of every piece of data we collect, where it goes, and what control you have over it. questions? write to support@entertheloop.app.

## What We Collect

loop collects only what you actively give it:

- Your name and email when you sign in
- The username you pick
- A profile picture, only if you upload one
- Your device timezone
- Every message you send to loop AI in chat
- Every post you publish to your loop
- Which posts you signal
- The loop requests you send and accept
- Reports you submit on posts
- Which dashboard cards you like or dislike

loop does NOT collect: your GPS location, camera roll, microphone, browsing history, or anything from other apps.

## Memory

loop's AI pays attention to what you say in chat and writes small facts to your private memory store — neighborhoods, restaurants, teams, tastes. Memory is private to your account. No other user can read it. You can wipe it at any time from the profile tab.

## How Your Data Leaves Loop

Supabase hosts your account, chats, memory, posts, and avatar. Data is encrypted at rest.

Anthropic (Claude AI) powers loop's AI. When you chat, your messages and memory snapshot are sent to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic does not use API requests to train its models by default.

Loop has no advertising business. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## Who Can See What

- Your chats: only you, ever
- Your memory: only you and the AI
- Your dashboard: only you
- Your loop posts: visible only to accepted friends, auto-deleted after 24 hours
- Your name, username, avatar: visible to anyone who searches your handle

## Your Controls

- Wipe memory: profile tab, menu, wipe memory
- Delete a post: long-press your post on the loop feed
- Remove a friend: friends screen, tap their avatar
- Delete your account: profile tab, menu, delete account — wipes everything permanently

## Data Retention

- Loop posts: auto-deleted 24 hours after publishing
- Chat messages: kept until you delete them or your account
- Memory: kept until you wipe it or delete your account
- Account: kept until you delete it

## Security

loop uses row-level security on the database. All traffic uses HTTPS. If you believe your account has been compromised, write to support@entertheloop.app.

## Age

loop is for users 18 and older. If you believe a minor is using loop, write to support@entertheloop.app.

## Changes

If anything material changes, the updated version will appear here and the date above will move.
