## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does Loop learn my preferences?**
Loop learns through your conversations with the AI and your feedback on places you visit. The more you use it the more accurate it gets.

**How do I edit what Loop knows about me?**
Open the Profile tab and go to Memory Manager to view, edit, or delete anything Loop has stored about you.

**How do I wipe my memory completely?**
Profile tab, menu, Wipe Memory. This cannot be undone.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile tab, menu, Delete Account. This permanently removes everything — chats, memory, posts, and your profile. Cannot be recovered.

**How do I remove a friend?**
Go to the Friends screen and tap their avatar in your Loop grid.

**My posts are not showing up for friends — why?**
Loop posts are visible only to accepted friends and are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

**I found a bug or something isn't working.**
Email support@entertheloop.app with a description of the issue and what device you're using. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.
