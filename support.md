## Common Questions

**How does Loop learn my preferences?**
Loop learns through your conversations with the AI and your feedback on places you visit.

**How do I delete my account?**
Email us at support@entertheloop.app and we'll delete your data within 48 hours.

**How do I update my preferences?**
Open the Profile tab and go to Memory Manager to view and edit everything Loop knows about you.
